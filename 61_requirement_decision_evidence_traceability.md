# ASKA v6 — Requirement, decision and evidence traceability

**Status:** proposed normative target. Requirements describe obligations; they do not certify an implemented machine.

## 1. Normative ownership and evidence semantics

Each `R-NN-XX` identifier is declared once in its owning chapter. Its operative text remains authoritative there. This register binds that requirement to an enforcer, assumptions, matching `T-NN-XX` acceptance oracle, observation scope, design decision and source basis. Requirements are not test results. An acceptance oracle states the consequence that must be demonstrated on a named implementation; it does not assert that equipment exists or that a campaign has run.

**Status SV** means “specified; complete implementation obligation unvalidated.” All realization-level requirements retain SV in this publication. The executed finite model and primitive checks in [Chapter 56](56_conformance_tests_and_vectors.md) provide limited supporting evidence for their stated abstractions. They do not promote the full corresponding hardware, distributed-protocol or operational requirements to an established status. Document consistency checks establish properties of this publication, not the proposed machine.

The architecture's top-level requirements are owned by [Chapter 02](02_requirements_and_threat_model.md). Detailed requirements refine those obligations without creating exceptions. The decision register D-01 through D-18 is owned by [Chapter 60](60_research_program_and_architectural_decisions.md). Source references are fully identified and linked in [Chapter 62](62_prior_art_sources_and_evidence_register.md). A source basis marked “design” means an ASKA normative choice; cited literature provides comparison or mechanism evidence only.

All rows have status SV unless a future configuration-specific dossier establishes a narrower claim explicitly.

## Normative requirements and acceptance obligations

**R-61-01 — Unique normative ownership.** Every requirement MUST have one canonical declaration and a complete traceability row.

**Acceptance T-61-01:** Compare declarations and rows bidirectionally; duplicate, missing or undefined identifiers fail publication acceptance.

**R-61-02 — Concrete enforcement.** Each row MUST identify an enforcer, explicit assumptions and an observable acceptance condition.

**Acceptance T-61-02:** A requirement justified only by intention, signature or unobservable logs is incomplete until an actual boundary and oracle are named.

**R-61-03 — Honest evidence state.** Requirements and proposed tests MUST NOT be labeled passed or implemented without configuration-bound evidence.

**Acceptance T-61-03:** A finite-model result cannot mark physical DMA, diode or sensor requirements established.

**R-61-04 — Change impact.** A change to a requirement, root, interface, schema or assumption MUST identify affected decisions, traces, tests and claims.

**Acceptance T-61-04:** Change a source-release rule or watcher signer; all affected rows and their evidence status are re-evaluated.

**R-61-05 — No orphaned guarantees.** Every claimed machine capability MUST map to a requirement and test or be clearly labeled a research hypothesis.

**Acceptance T-61-05:** A claimed automatic recovery, private telemetry or physical independence guarantee without an enforcing/test row fails review.
